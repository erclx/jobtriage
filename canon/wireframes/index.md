# Wireframes

Per-surface ASCII layouts loaded on demand

- [BYOK gate](byok-gate.md): First surface every visitor sees
- [Canvas views](canvas-views.md): The view switcher chips at the top of the canvas drive the layout
- [Chat header](chat-header.md): Spans the top of the workspace
- [Chat rail conversation](chat-rail-conversation.md): Shown in the left rail after at least one assistant turn has rendered
- [Empty workspace](empty-workspace.md): Shown after the gate is passed and before the first message
- [Engagement status row](engagement-status-row.md): Renders inline in the chat rail trace for trackStatus
- [Export shortlist](export-shortlist.md): Toolbar affordance pinned to the right end of the canvas view switcher
- [Feature name](feature-name.md): Brief sentence describing when and where the surface appears.
- [Input row](input-row.md): Always at the bottom of the chat rail
- [Intentional omissions](intentional-omissions.md): Surfaces deliberately excluded so they are not re-added later
- [Mobile layout](mobile-layout.md): Below-lg fallback for every surface
- [Mock mode chat](mock-mode-chat.md): Shown when the visitor picks Try the demo, no key from the BYOK gate
- [Profile dialog](profile-dialog.md): Opens from the header profile button
- [Profile node](profile-node.md): Anchored to the top-left of every canvas view, never deleted
- [Tool error](tool-error.md): Renders when a tool call fails (input validation, upstream error, output validation)
- [Voice input](voice-input.md): Mic affordance in the chat input row, Chrome-only
- [Workspace layout](workspace-layout.md): Two columns above 1024px wide
